\begin{center}\small

Manual & \bw & What changed \\
\midrule
M1 & 1.5.0 & First version: the skeleton; \cref{ch:burning-cell}, the
  burning cell; \cref{ch:burn-models}, the six burn models (HVRB, the lead

  over bins of initial position, from the atmospheric table and a
  counter-based generator), and its verification. \\
M1 & 1.8.0 & HALO built: \cref{sec:halo} from ``under development'' to as
  built: activation by compression work, new compression only (DK), hot
  spots stepped exactly and never reactivated, the burn as a potential,
  $K_B$ for the planned $\tau_b$; its verification and its desensitization
  beside HVRB. \\
M1 & 1.9.0 & CLAUDE built, the last of the six: \cref{sec:claude} from
  ``under development'' to as built: activation by the cell's entropy, a
  state function (the review's F-12 answered), centers born through the
  step, the quench time $\tau_q$ (DK), its verification and its
  desensitization beside HALO and HVRB. \\
M2 & after 1.9.0 & The book reorganized from the physics to the code
  (\code{doc/design/manual.md}, revised outline): \cref{ch:continuum}, one-dimensional
  continuum mechanics, \cref{ch:shocks}, shock waves, and
  \cref{ch:method-av}, method AV, written;
  \cref{app:hugoniot}, Hugoniot data for the elements, planned; the
  burning cell and the burn models now chapters 7 and 8. \\
