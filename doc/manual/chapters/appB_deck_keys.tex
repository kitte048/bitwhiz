\chapter{Deck keys of the burn}
\label{app:deck-keys}

The input deck is described in full in \code{input/README.md}, which owns
it; this appendix only maps the symbols of \cref{ch:burning-cell,ch:burn-models}
to their keys. Keys are case-insensitive and sit in the Material block. A
burn key that is not the material's model's is a deck error, as is any burn
key without a burn.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l l Y@{}}
\toprule
Key & Symbol & Units in the deck & Used by \\
\midrule
\key{burn} & -- & \key{hvrb}, \key{thermalcascade}, \key{flashfire},
  \key{stochasticignite} & a material that burns \\
\key{eos} & -- & \key{mie_gruneisen_t} & required with a burn \\
\key{CV}, \key{T0} & $\Cv$, $T_0$ & J/(g\,K), K & the reactant \\
\key{products} & -- & \key{ideal_gas} & required with a burn \\
\key{GAMMA}, \key{Q} & $\gamma$, $Q$ & --, kJ/g & the products \\
\midrule
\key{PR} & $P_R$ & GPa & HVRB, ThermalCascade, FlashFire, StochasticIgnite (and every planned model) \\
\key{ZR}, \key{MR}, \key{XR} & $Z_R$, $M_R$, $X_R$ & -- & HVRB \\
\key{PI}, \key{TAU0} & $P_I$, $\tau_0$ & GPa, \textmu s & HVRB \\
\key{HT} & $H_T$ & 1/(\textmu s\,K$^{G_T}$) & ThermalCascade \\
\key{TC}, \key{GT} & $T_c$, $G_T$ & K, -- & ThermalCascade \\
\key{NS} & $N_S$ & -- & ThermalCascade, FlashFire, StochasticIgnite \\
\key{KFF} & $K_F$ & 1/\textmu s & FlashFire \\
\key{AFF}, \key{BFF} & $A_F$, $B_F$ & -- & FlashFire \\
\key{TA} & $T_a$ & K & FlashFire \\
\key{LAM0} & $\lam_0$ & -- (between 0 and 1) & FlashFire \\
\key{MU}, \key{SIGMA} & $\mu$, $\sigma$ & 1/\textmu s & StochasticIgnite \\
\key{AVRAMI} & $a$ & -- & StochasticIgnite \\
\key{LC}, \key{TAUZ} & $\ell_c$, $\tau_Z$ & mm, \textmu s & StochasticIgnite \\
\key{SEED} & -- & a whole number, 0 to 19999 & StochasticIgnite \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The deck's \key{CV} is in J/(g\,K); the code divides it by 1000, to
kJ/(g\,K), so that $\Cv T$ is in kJ/g. The planned models' keys are in their
sections of \cref{ch:burn-models}.
